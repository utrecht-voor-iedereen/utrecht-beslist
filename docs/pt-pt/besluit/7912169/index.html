Multi-Year Spatial Perspective 2026

💡 Utrecht residents can expect better spatial development.

🎯 🎯 Contexto e Razões da Proposta

📋 💡 Resumo em Linguagem Clara (B1)

The college of B and W proposes to the council to adopt the Multi-Year Spatial Perspective 2026. This perspective outlines how the municipality of Utrecht will deal with space in the coming years.
