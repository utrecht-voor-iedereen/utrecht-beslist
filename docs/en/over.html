About Utrecht Beslist & Data Retention

Transparent methodology concerning public open data collection, Woo rights, and permanent archiving policy.

What is Utrecht Beslist?

Utrecht Beslist is an independent, open-source civic project that automatically collects and summarizes Utrecht city council documents into plain language (B1 level in 8 languages).

💡 100% Open Data & Transparency: All data is sourced from public government APIs. Every summary includes a direct link to the original official document.

📜 Data Retention & Permanent Archiving (Archives Act)

Pursuant to the Dutch Archives Act 1995 and the Open Government Act (Woo), council decisions are permanently preserved and never deleted.
