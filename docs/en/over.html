About Utrecht Beslist & Data Retention

Transparent methodology concerning public open data collection, Woo rights, and permanent archiving policy.

What is Utrecht Beslist?

Utrecht Beslist is an independent, open-source civic project that automatically collects and summarizes Utrecht city council documents into plain language (B1 Dutch and English).

💡 100% Open Data & Transparency: All data is sourced from public government APIs. Every summary includes a direct link to the original council document, audio TTS narration, dark mode, and PDF printing.

📜 Data Retention & Permanent Archiving (Archives Act)

How long do documents stay on the website?

Pursuant to the Dutch Archives Act 1995 and the Open Government Act (Woo), council decisions and municipal proposals are public historical facts. Documents are permanently preserved in the repository and are never deleted.

To keep the main homepage fast and accessible, the primary view displays recent decisions, while all historical decisions remain permanently searchable via monthly archives (e.g. /en/archief/2026/07/) and the public JSON API (../data/latest.json).

🌐 Official Sources & Woo Rights

Utrecht Beslist interfaces directly with official government open data portals:

  • Open Raadsinformatie (ORI): openraadsinformatie.nl — National platform for municipal open council documents.
  • Municipality of Utrecht Council Information: utrecht.bestuurlijkeinformatie.nl — Official council portal of Utrecht.
  • Open Government Act (Woo): Citizens have the legal right to request additional background government records or annexes directly from the Municipality of Utrecht.

🔄 How does the automated pipeline work?

1. Daily Fetch (06:00 UTC)

Automated GitHub Actions workflows query the Open Raadsinformatie ElasticSearch API daily for new Utrecht council documents.

2. Automatic Filtering

Administrative routine files (such as attendance lists) are filtered out automatically to retain only substantive council proposals.

3. AI B1 Translation

Open-weights LLMs (Groq / Llama 3) translate administrative jargon into accessible B1 plain language without altering legal meaning.

4. Static Publishing

The site compiles into 100% static HTML pages with a public open JSON API (latest.json), requiring zero cookies or tracking.
