Exemption of residence requirement

💡 Utrecht residents may potentially get an alderman who does not live in Utrecht.

🎯 Context & Background

This proposal was submitted to offer more opportunities for finding suitable candidates for the alderman position.

📋 Decision & Agreements

The city council discusses a proposal to exempt the residence requirement for aldermen. This means that someone who does not live in Utrecht can still become an alderman.

  • The proposal is to exempt the residence requirement for aldermen.
  • This council proposal affects all residents of Utrecht.
  • There are no extra costs associated with this proposal.

🏘️ Impact on Residents

It is possible that an alderman who does not live in Utrecht may be less connected to the local community.
