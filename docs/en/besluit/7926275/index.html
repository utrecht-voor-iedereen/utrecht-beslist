Environmental plan update Pijlsweerd, Lauwerecht, Vogelenbuurt, Tuinwijk

💡 Existing building rules for Pijlsweerd, Lauwerecht, Vogelenbuurt, Staatsliedenbuurt, and Tuinwijk are converted to the new Omgevingswet framework.

💡 Plain Language Summary (B1)

The council is adopting the second amendment to the Utrecht environmental plan. This converts ten zoning plans in Noordwest and Noordoost into the definitive environmental plan and adds rules on soil quality and archaeology.

  • 📌 Conversion of 10 zoning plans and temporary rules into the definitive Utrecht environmental plan.
  • 👥 Residents and homeowners in Pijlsweerd, Lauwerecht, Vogelenbuurt, Staatsliedenbuurt, and Tuinwijk.
  • 💶 The document states no specific financial amounts.

🎯 Background & Context

Since the Omgevingswet took effect in 2024, all temporary zoning plans must be definitively converted before 2032.

🏘️ Consequences & City Changes

Updated rules for soil quality and archaeology apply city-wide; the transition has no direct impact on trees or greenery.
